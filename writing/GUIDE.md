# Application writing

Draft only what the form asks for. Read the live application step before writing. A public page that has no letter field does not need a letter. An optional message can stay unused. A later step you have not seen is unknown, not empty.

## Length

About 60–100 words for an optional message. About 80–140 words for an experience answer, unless the person or the form sets another limit. Do not fill a large character limit just because it exists.

## Facts

Use `profile/PROFILE.md` and `cv/interview-brief.md`. If a form asks for an example the brief does not contain, mark it **Needs their input** and give the structure, not a fictional story.

Keep these separate when a form asks for money:

- contractor invoice rate
- employee gross
- take-home net

Name the currency, period, and basis. Do not paste a net monthly floor into a field that asks for annual gross.

## Voice

Match `writing.tone` and `profile/voice.md` when it exists. Direct, specific, and free of filler. No invented metrics. No confidential internal names in anything that would be submitted.

## Employer rules

Some employers require answers the candidate writes themselves, or ban generated application text. When the posting says that, collect the facts and the outline, and leave the submitted prose to the person. Say that this is why there is no paste-ready answer.

## After drafting

Label every draft as unsent. Quote the field it belongs in. Do not submit it, and do not put it in a mailbox draft, unless the current message asks for that.
