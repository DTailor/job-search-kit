# Profile and fit model

Written from the setup interview on YYYY-MM-DD. Facts below are ones the person confirmed. Open questions stay listed. This file does not claim a fresh market check.

## Who they are

Headline, level, location, and a short summary of the work they actually do.

## Evidence

**Core.** Skills and responsibilities they can defend with recent work.

**Adjacent.** Usable, and not the headline.

**Do not claim.** Stale skills, missing expertise, and anything they refused to imply.

**Domains.** Industries where their history is directly credible.

## What fits

Ordered. A stretch is a stretch: say what would have to be true for it to be worth a screen.

## What does not fit

Role types, schedules, locations, and named companies to leave out.

## Scoring

Use the weights in `profile.yml`. A role with any `fail` gate is rejected or listed under rejected, even if the weighted score looks high. Missing pay scores as unknown, not as zero and not as an estimate.

## Vehicles

Ways they can be engaged, ways they cannot, and what they need in writing for leave, notice, payment, and the client. Do not restate this as a tax model.

## Markets
