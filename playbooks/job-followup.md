# job-followup: draft application follow-ups

Read this workspace's AGENTS.md and playbooks/README.md first, then `profile/PREFERENCES.md` (Writing, availability, Workflow notes > job-followup). Resolve paths relative to the workspace root unless an external location is explicitly named.

Argument: `[days | company]` (default: the follow-up threshold in `profile/PREFERENCES.md`, else 10 days). Use the user's accompanying request; do not treat literal template placeholders as arguments.

Rules: drafts only. Never send, never create mailbox drafts, never post on a professional network without the person's explicit OK for that specific message. Writing style: direct, professional, modest; no "I hope this finds you well", no pressure.

1. From the live tracker, take Applied / Asked screening Qs records (pipeline and Shortlist). Get the applied date from Notes or `<paths.applications>/*/application.md`. Refresh the relevant threads if their status is stale; do not force a full pipeline sweep just to draft a focused follow-up.
2. For each application quiet for N or more days: find the contact route (recruiter email from the confirmation or a reply, the ATS reply address, or a known recruiter on the professional network; say when there is none). Draft a follow-up of 3-5 sentences: role, date applied, one concrete fit line taken from posting.md, one open question (contract route from the person's country, or timeline), and the person's availability wording from `profile/PREFERENCES.md`.
3. For applications quiet for the withdrawal threshold (default 60 days): propose Status `Withdrawn` (no response), with a note. Change it only after approval.
4. Output: a table (company, days quiet, route) plus each draft, ready to paste.
