# job-interview: prepare for an interview

Read this workspace's AGENTS.md and playbooks/README.md first, then `profile/PROFILE.md` (positioning, story map, materials) and `profile/PREFERENCES.md` (Workflow notes > job-interview). Resolve paths relative to the workspace root unless an external location is explicitly named.

Argument: `<company> [stage, date, interviewer]`. Use the user's accompanying request; do not treat literal template placeholders as arguments.

Rules: follow the positioning in `profile/PROFILE.md` (what the person is, what not to claim); no invented metrics; no customer, colleague or internal names. Verify company claims from primary sources and cite them.

1. Load `<paths.applications>/<company>_*/` (posting.md, application.md), the tracker row (fit, risks, contract route, pay), the interview brief and current CV listed in `profile/PROFILE.md` (Materials). Use the latest confirmed stage/date/format/interviewer from the request or application evidence. Ask only for missing details that matter, and continue independent preparation meanwhile.
2. Use or refresh the dated [company-background.md](company-background.md) dossier before interview or substantial-assessment preparation; research employer, agency and client separately. Summarize the material product/stability/reputation/contract findings and source-linked questions in prep.md, linking the reusable dossier rather than duplicating it. Keep team/stack signals and relevant compensation evidence attributed.
3. Write `prep.md` in the application folder, at most 2 pages:
   - Likely questions for this stage: the posting's must-haves, and gaps from the tracker's risk column, each with an honest bridge answer.
   - Story map: which of the person's stories (listed in `profile/PROFILE.md`, generalised, no confidential names) answers which question, in 2-3 lines each.
   - Refresh: the 3-5 skills or topics most likely to come up, linked to any refresh material listed in `profile/PROFILE.md`, and which portfolio pieces or case studies to show.
   - Money and logistics: the rate or band already given (application.md), contract-route position, country eligibility, availability wording, after-hours position.
   - 5 questions to ask, specific to this company.
4. Offer a mock interview: one question at a time, short feedback after each answer, and a score at the end.
