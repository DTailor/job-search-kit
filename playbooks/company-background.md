# Company background — required for retained job leads

Apply this before presenting a retained role, recommending an application or prioritizing further interview/assessment effort. Raw search cards discarded during triage are not retained findings. Check each newly retained recruiter inbound as well as scout picks. No separate skill invocation is needed. Apply `profile/PREFERENCES.md` (Workflow notes > company-background) if present.

## One company dossier, role-specific conclusions

Save `<paths.companies>/<company-slug>.md` with checked date, exact domain/legal identifiers, source links, findings, gaps, judgement and related role URLs. Reuse the dossier across requisitions; add a separate section when the entity, client or role terms differ. Review source currency before reuse; refresh material news/reputation/identity at least every 30 days while actively considering the company, and again before a substantial take-home or offer decision. A new funding, acquisition, insolvency, payment or layoff signal triggers an earlier refresh. State the financial reporting period separately from the check date.

For an agency/network, distinguish the recruiter, signing/paying entity and actual client. Verify the client separately when disclosed; never guess it. Label an undisclosed client as a coverage gap and assess project/bench/early-termination risk. Do not treat agency admission as a paid placement.

## What to check

1. **Identity and business:** correct company/domain, legal name, jurisdiction/registration, active/dissolved/insolvency status where publicly accessible; founders/ownership and parent links when material; what the product/services are, stage and real customer/product evidence. Resolve namesakes and brand/entity mismatches before attributing results. Match the proposed counterparty rather than assuming the brand owner will sign.
2. **Financial stability:** latest available filed accounts, revenue/profit or loss, cash/debt and going-concern qualifications where accessible; financing/acquisitions and whether announced funding is actually new cash. Record dates, currencies, reporting entity and company versus group basis. An old round, valuation, assets under management or client money is not current operating runway. Do not manufacture runway from cash divided by an incomplete profit figure.
3. **Employment and recruitment reputation:** recent employee/contractor and candidate experiences; repeated patterns in management, workload, turnover, layoffs, payment, assessment burden and hiring freezes. Distinguish employee reviews from customer-service ratings and agency-client testimonials. Record platform, review date/sample size/role/location where available; anonymous allegations are signals to question, not established misconduct. Check contrary evidence and company responses when material.
4. **Material adverse news and conduct:** targeted searches for layoffs, restructuring/insolvency, unpaid contractors, litigation/regulatory action and credible reporting of serious misconduct. Attribute allegations accurately and read outcomes, not just complaint headlines. Use official registers/regulators/court decisions when available. Record bounded searches and access gaps; do not describe this as a comprehensive legal, sanctions or credit audit.
5. **The person's practical risk:** the contract route from the person's country (forms listed in `profile/PREFERENCES.md`), country-approved compensation, working hours and after-hours duties; named client, actual work, minimum term, leave, notice/payment, exclusivity, liability/IP and continuity. Separate advertised terms from recruiter statements and signed/accepted terms. Flag unusually broad recruitment/data terms when actually observed; do not tick or change consents.

## Evidence and output

Prefer public official registers/accounts, company legal documents, investor announcements and original credible reporting. Search reviews/news as a separate track; company marketing alone is insufficient background evidence. If a register or review site is blocked, use an attributed secondary source if useful and say the underlying record was not independently inspected. Do not create accounts, pay for reports, contact people or transmit the person's information for this research without instruction.

Each dossier should make these easy to find:

- **Judgement:** `Continue`, `Screen before investing`, `Hold` or `Avoid`, with the concrete reason and confidence/coverage stated in words. This is employment suitability, not a credit rating or guarantee.
- **Established / claimed / reported / unknown:** source-linked findings across the five areas, including adverse findings and contradictions.
- **Questions before proceeding:** a short prioritized list tied to the unresolved risks.
- **Coverage:** what was actually read/searched, check date, financial period, inaccessible sources and unresolved identities/client information.

Every scout/plan/inbound table must include a brief **Company background / main risk** cell or nearby concise finding, and link the dossier. Do not hide a material finding only in a long appendix. Record dossier path, checked date and judgement in findings data as a backwards-compatible companion field/object; preserve existing required schemas and explicitly distinguish pending checks. A role with an incomplete background check may remain a labelled research lead, but must not be presented as vetted.

Keep company facts separate from technical-fit and application-stage decisions. Background concerns can change application priority, but neither positive reviews nor funding clear country/pay/after-hours gates. No outgoing messages, applications or consent changes are authorized by this check. Record findings and their practical effect in the tracker (under standing authority, else as a proposal); incomplete coverage stays explicit.
