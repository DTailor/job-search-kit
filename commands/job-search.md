# Search

Find currently open roles. Argument: $ARGUMENTS (a slice such as "backend, EU remote" or "these companies"). If the argument is empty, propose two or three slices from the profile and ask which to run.

1. Read `profile/profile.yml`, `profile/PROFILE.md`, and `AGENTS.md`. If the profile is not `ready`, follow `SETUP.md` and stop.
2. Read `search/SEARCH_BRIEF.md` and `search/schema.json`.
3. Search that slice only. Save `search/runs/YYYY-MM-DD/<slice>.json` as you go, then `search/runs/YYYY-MM-DD/BRIEF.md`.
4. Append new qualifying roles to `tracker/roles.csv`. Match on URL. Leave rows at `Applied` or later unchanged.
5. Reply with counts, the top roles, the failed gates behind anything you dropped, and what still needs a browser or a human check.

Do not apply or message anyone in this procedure.
