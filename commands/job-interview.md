# Interview prep

Argument: $ARGUMENTS (company, and stage, date, or interviewer when they know it).

1. Load `applications/<company>_*/`, the tracker row, `cv/interview-brief.md`, and the profile. Ask for stage, date, format, and interviewer when they are missing.
2. Research the company from primary sources: product, stage, stack signals, recent news. Cite them.
3. Write `prep.md` from `templates/prep.md` in the application folder. Keep it to about two pages.
4. Offer a mock: one question at a time, short feedback after each answer, and a score at the end. Wait before asking the first question.

Do not invent metrics or stories. A gap in the brief stays a gap.
