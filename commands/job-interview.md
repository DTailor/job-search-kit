# Interview prep

The person names a company, and the stage, date, or interviewer when they know it.

1. Load `applications/<company>_*/`, the tracker row, `cv/interview-brief.md`, `cv/evidence-ledger.md`, and the profile. Ask for stage, date, format, and interviewer when they are missing.
2. Research the company from primary sources: product, stage, stack signals, recent news. Cite them. Use `tracker/companies.csv` when that company was already checked, and say how old the check is.
3. Write `prep.md` from `templates/prep.md` in the application folder. Keep it to about two pages. Map questions only to stories in the brief or the ledger. A missing story stays a gap.
4. Include the recruiter-screen checks from the interview brief: actual team, hands-on share of the work, contracting entity, duration, notice, leave, hours, on-call, and pay.
5. Offer a mock: one question at a time, short feedback after each answer, and a score at the end. Wait before asking the first question.

Do not invent metrics or stories.
